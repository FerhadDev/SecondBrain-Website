# 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm **Farhad Zamanov**, a **Game Developer & Game Designer** and Computer Engineering student at Azerbaijan State Oil and Industry University (ASOIU).

My main focus is **gameplay programming and game mechanics**. I enjoy designing engaging gameplay experiences, developing interactive systems, and transforming creative ideas into playable games.

I primarily work with **Unity and C#**, and I also have experience exploring Unreal Engine.

I'm passionate about indie game development, experimenting with new mechanics, solving technical challenges, and collaborating with other developers.

## 🎮 Game Development Experience

I have around two years of experience in game development, including personal projects, collaborative projects, and game jams.

I'm involved with **Hyperarc**, an indie game development team where I collaborate with programmers, game designers, and artists.

My main development interests and experience include:

- Gameplay programming and mechanics
- Game design and prototyping
- Unity 2D and 3D development
- Player interaction systems
- Game architecture and reusable systems
- UI and gameplay flow
- Event-driven programming
- Rapid prototyping during game jams
- Team collaboration and project development

I enjoy working on both the technical and creative sides of game development, especially designing mechanics and implementing the systems that make them work.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### [Are you king enough](brain/Projects/Game-Jam/Are%20you%20king%20enough.md)

**Genre:** Decision-Making / Resource Management

A game where players take the role of a king and make decisions about requests from their people while managing limited resources.

Each decision can influence the kingdom and change the gameplay experience.

The project was developed during a game jam, where I contributed to gameplay development and team coordination.

**Technologies:** Unity, C#, DOTween

[Download on itch.io](https://farhaddev.itch.io/are-you-king-enough)

### [Lucid Space](brain/Projects/Game-Jam/Lucid%20Space.md)

A Windows puzzle prototype by HyperArc Studios, developed for GameponsJam
Vol. 3 over 2 days with a 2D pixel-art, top-down style. My role was Game Developer.

[View on itch.io](https://hyperarcstudios.itch.io/lucid-space)

### [Redactor](brain/Projects/Team/Redactor.md)

**Genre:** Decision-Based / Political Simulation

Redactor is a game development project focused on examining books and making publication decisions under government rules.

Players must evaluate incoming books and make decisions that affect the game world.

The project explores decision-making, resource management, and the consequences of player actions.

**Engine:** Unity
