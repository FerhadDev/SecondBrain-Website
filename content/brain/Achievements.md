# Achievements

## [Green Energy Hackathon — 1st place](brain/Achievements/Green%20Energy%20Hackathon%202026%20-%20First%20Place.md)

In April 2026, my team, **HelioSight**, won first place at the Green Energy Hackathon at Yessenov University in Aktau, Kazakhstan. We built a prototype that used AI to track clouds and adjust the orientation of solar panels.

## [Turkic Game Jam 2025 — 4th place](brain/Achievements/Turkic%20Game%20Jam%202025%20-%20Fourth%20Place.md)

Our team built [Tengri](brain/Projects/Game-Jam/Tengri.md) in two days and placed fourth. We later represented Azerbaijan at a conference involving five Turkic countries.

My role was **Game Developer**. I built the board system, piece movement, combat, and main-menu button logic.

## [Gamepons Compass III Demo Day — Project showcase](brain/Achievements/Gamepons%20Compass%20III%20Demo%20Day%20-%20Project%20Showcase.md)

We presented **Redactor** on December 25, 2025. This was a project showcase milestone.
