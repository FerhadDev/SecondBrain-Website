# Redactor

## Overview

Redactor focuses on examining books and making publication decisions under government rules. Players evaluate incoming books and make decisions that affect the game world. The project explores decision-making, resource management, and the consequences of player actions.

## My contributions

I worked on the book inspection interface and stamping interactions.

## Project showcase

We presented Redactor on December 25, 2025 at Gamepons Compass III Demo Day.
