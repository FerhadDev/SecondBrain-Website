# Blue Diary

Details confirmed by me, except where marked unverified or unknown.

## Overview

- **Team:** No Studio.
- **Context:** Team project developed during an incubation program. The program name is not confirmed.
- **Period:** I worked on it around 2024; exact dates are not confirmed.
- **Genre:** 2.5D pixel art adventure and puzzle game.
- **Role:** Developer.
- **Engine:** Unity. I remember the version as 6000.0.48f1, but this is unverified.
- **Status:** Discontinued. Our team stopped development and has no plans to resume it.

## My contribution

I built puzzles, a playable in-game piano designed to feel realistic, and a music player with discs.

## Story

Mövlud worked in a government job. On his 65th birthday, he returns home and finds his daughter Ziver’s diary in the attic. Her entries reveal painful memories of her father. Mövlud revisits those moments and tries to put things right.
