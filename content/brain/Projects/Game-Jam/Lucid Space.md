# Lucid Space

## Project overview

Lucid Space is a Windows puzzle prototype by Trojan Horse Studios, created for
GameponsJam Vol. 3 over 2 days. The design board describes it as a
single-player, top-down 2.5D puzzle game with pixel art, made in Unity for PC.
My role was Game Developer.

These details were supplied by me; the linked project page has not been
independently checked for this note.

## Game Jam information

- Event: GameponsJam Vol. 3.
- Duration: 2 days.
- Theme: It works, but it shouldn't.
- Results or awards: 4th place

## Gameplay

- Genre: Puzzle.
- Style: Top-down 2.5D with pixel art.
- Mode: Single-player.
- Platform: PC (Windows).

Space pirates have taken over your ship. To take it back, you explore
the ship, collect music discs, and play them on a portable music player.
Different discs give you different abilities. Use those abilities to solve
puzzles and unlock doors as you work your way through the ship.

**Core loop:** Enter a new area → use a disc ability to solve a puzzle
→ unlock the next door → progress toward reclaiming the ship.

Source: User-supplied gameplay description and design-board summary. The
design-board description does not confirm implementation of draft features.
