# Tengri

## Overview

Tengri is a chess-inspired 2D strategy board game by HyperArc Studio for two players sharing one PC. Every piece uses the same movement pattern: one tile per move. Players spend energy on a combination of moves, fights, and cards, with the goal of defeating the opposing queen.

The Windows prototype is playable. Development has stopped.

## Gameplay

Each player has **5 energy per turn**. Players can combine actions and use multiple pieces during the same turn as long as they have enough energy.

| Action | Energy cost | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move a piece one tile | 1 | Available energy |
| Fight | 2 | Available energy |
| Draw a card | 1 | One draw per turn |
| Play a card | Cost shown on the card | Available energy |

## My contributions

As a Game Developer, I implemented:

- The chess-style board system.
- Piece movement.
- Combat.
- Main-menu button logic.

The team's art and game design are credited separately below.

## Game jam and project result

We built Tengri during **Turkic Game Jam 2025** over **two days**, and our team placed **4th**.

After the jam, we continued developing the prototype to make it more fun and playable. We later represented Azerbaijan at a conference involving five Turkic countries. Development has since stopped.
